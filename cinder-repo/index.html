What makes Cinder different

  • Zero-knowledge. Encryption happens in your browser. Storage only ever holds ciphertext + the decryption key lives in the link itself, never on the server.
  • Read receipts. You get a private code that tells you if and when your secret was opened — peace of mind no other tool gives you.
  • Flexible burn rules. Destroy after N reads, after a timer, or both. Add a passphrase for a true second factor.
  • It actually burns. A small, satisfying moment when the secret turns to ash.
